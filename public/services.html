What we do, and how we approach each of it

Everything here is quoted after somebody has looked at it. That is not a way of avoiding the question: it is because the difference between a straightforward installation and an awkward one is a pipe route and a condenser position, and neither can be judged from a photograph of a room, let alone from a phone call.

What we can tell you before any of that is how we approach each kind of work, what usually goes wrong with it, and what moves the price. Each page below does that for one thing we do. Where a job is really a different job wearing the same name, the pages say so: cooling will not fix condensation, a regas will not fix a leak, and a system sized from floor area will disappoint you in the second summer.

Air Conditioning Installation

Wall-mounted split air conditioning installed by F-Gas registered engineers across London and the South East. Free quote visit, fixed written price, warranty registered for you.

Air Conditioning Repairs and Regas

Not cooling, leaking, tripping out or noisy. Leak detection, component replacement and recharge to the correct weighed-in charge, by F-Gas registered engineers.

Air Conditioning Servicing

Annual and six-monthly air conditioning servicing across London and the South East. Filter and coil cleaning, drain treatment, refrigerant and electrical checks, readings recorded.

Heating and Air to Air Heat Pumps

Modern inverter air conditioning is a reversible air to air heat pump. Sized for the heating load as well as the cooling load, installed across London and the South East.

Commercial Air Conditioning

Offices, retail, hospitality, gyms and comms rooms across London and the South East. Out of hours installation, F-Gas leak checking and logging, priority breakdown response.

Multi-Split Systems

Multi-split systems serving two to five rooms from one outdoor unit. Sized on simultaneous load, installed and commissioned by F-Gas registered engineers across the South East.

Ventilation and MVHR

Extract ventilation, positive input ventilation and whole-house MVHR installed and commissioned across London and the South East by CoolRight.
